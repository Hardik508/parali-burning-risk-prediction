# Data sources, libraries, references and disclosure

## Data (all public)

- **NASA FIRMS, VIIRS active fire detections**: fire labels and fire history. https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov (NASA open data policy).
- **Sentinel-2 Surface Reflectance** (Copernicus programme, ESA): image chips, NDVI, NDMI. Accessed through Google Earth Engine. Free and open (Copernicus licence).
- **ERA5-Land** (Copernicus Climate Change Service / ECMWF): daily weather. Contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information; accessed through Google Earth Engine.
- **ESA WorldCover**: land-cover mask for cropland.
- Google Earth Engine: https://earthengine.google.com (used under its non-commercial/research terms).

## Libraries

PyTorch, NumPy, pandas, scikit-learn, matplotlib, folium, joblib, earthengine-api, geemap (see requirements.txt).
